Privacy

Pesaby provides tools for checkout, payments, stock, reporting and business operations. This page explains what information is processed when you use Pesaby and what is expected from everyone using the service.

When a business uses Pesaby, that business controls the customer, sales and inventory information entered into its workspace. Pesaby processes that information to provide, support and improve the service.

Information we collect

Account information

Name, work email, phone number, role and login details used to create and manage a Pesaby workspace.

Business information

Business name, locations, products, team members, settings and subscription details supplied by your organization.

Operational data

Sales, payments, stock movements, reports and audit activity created when your team uses the platform.

Technical data

Device, browser, IP address, log and diagnostic data used to keep Pesaby reliable and secure.

How we use information

  • Provide checkout, inventory, reporting and workspace features.
  • Set up accounts, respond to support requests and communicate service updates.
  • Protect the platform, investigate misuse and maintain reliable performance.
  • Improve Pesaby using aggregated or de-identified usage information.

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to run Pesaby, when required by law, or when you instruct us to do so.

Security & retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect Pesaby data. Access is limited to people and providers who need it to operate or support the platform.

We retain information for as long as your account is active and as needed for legitimate business, security, legal and accounting purposes. Workspace owners can request account assistance or data export through Pesaby support.

Terms of use

Your Pesaby account

You are responsible for keeping account credentials secure and for the activity of people you invite to your workspace. Provide accurate details and notify us promptly if you believe there has been unauthorized access.

Acceptable use

Use Pesaby only for lawful business purposes. Do not interfere with the service, attempt to access another organization's data, introduce harmful code, or use the platform to violate the rights of others.

Your data

You retain ownership of the business information you enter into Pesaby. You give Pesaby permission to host, process and display that information only as needed to provide the service to you.

Service changes

We may update Pesaby, these terms or this privacy notice as the service evolves. Material changes will be communicated through the service or by email where appropriate. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms.

QUESTIONS?

Talk to Pesaby.

For questions about privacy, your account or these terms, contact our team and include your business name so we can help quickly.
